Press and hold a key to sound it in the chosen tuning, built from the tonic of your key. Tinted keys are the tonic. On a computer keyboard, A is C4 and the keys from A to K climb by semitone from there, with W, E, T, Y and U for the black keys. Press Z or X, or use the octave buttons, to move those keys down or up an octave. Scroll sideways for the full range, A0 to A8.
